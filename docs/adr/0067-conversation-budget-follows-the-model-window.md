# The Conversation Budget Follows the Model Window and Folding Becomes a Safety Net

`activeContext.maxConversationBytes` has been 128KB, roughly thirty thousand tokens, since the budget widened from observation bytes to the whole conversation array in ADR 0019. V4 Flash and V4 Pro carry a 1M-token context, so Forgelet has been folding at about three percent of the available window. ADR 0019 accepted repeated lossy re-encoding of the Rolling Summary narrative because a long Session had no other way to stay inside the window; that justification no longer holds at thirty thousand tokens. We decided the conversation budget follows the model's window rather than a historical constant: 512KB for coding, 256KB for learning, and 128KB for writing, set through the per-stage `maxConversationBytes` override that `RoutingConfig` already carries. Folding keeps every part of its machinery — hysteresis, protected turns, the Fact Ledger, the Degraded Fold — and changes status only: it becomes the safety net a long Session may never touch instead of the routine path a long Session always takes.

The argument is fidelity, not money, and we record that explicitly because the cache economics look more persuasive than they are. Folding rewrites the head of the conversation and so reforks the cached prefix ADR 0032 works to preserve, which re-bills the whole context at DeepSeek's cache-miss rate, fifty times the cache-hit rate; ADR 0032 accepted that cost "for now" and deferred evaluating it, and this is that evaluation. But the absolute numbers are small — about a cent per fold against a 128KB context, against roughly four hundredths of a cent per turn to carry a 512KB context on cache hits — so cost alone would not justify the change. What justifies it is that every fold is an irreversible lossy re-encoding of the Session's own reasoning, and there is no longer any technical reason to accept that loss so early.

Two consequences are deliberate. ADR 0065's uncompactable Provider Carryover would, at 128KB with `max` effort, plausibly push the protected recent turns past the budget on their own, and ADR 0019 answers that case by stopping the Session — a death Forgelet cannot fold its way out of; the raised budget moves that cliff an order of magnitude away. And an unbounded single turn can now exceed the whole budget by itself, since the models emit up to 384K tokens, so a per-Workflow output ceiling derived from this budget becomes necessary rather than optional — and, because `finish_reason` is currently recorded but never branched on, truncation must be made observable before any ceiling is imposed, or a truncated answer stays indistinguishable from a complete one.
